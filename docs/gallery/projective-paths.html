Through the pole

Move along a projective line in two channels. The rings show the point itself. Each readout chooses a local coordinate and carries its tangent through the change of chart.

Each ring represents the real projective line. Its right edge is [1 : 0], the affine point at infinity. Near that edge, y/x passes smoothly through zero. On an overlap, the coordinates are reciprocals and their tangents obey the derivative of the reciprocal.
