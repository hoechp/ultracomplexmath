What squares to one?

A sign can change in either split channel. Try the idempotent (1+j)/2 to free an entire tangent direction.

One trace, three fields

All three fields start with (1−x²)² and the same normal slope x/2. Move away from y = 0 and watch their cross-sections separate.

● Harmonic · i² = −1● Wave · j² = +1● Shear · ε² = 0

Two straight lines through a saddle

Fix one channel and vary the other. Each traces a straight line on z = ab. Varying both with unit speed produces the violet tangent arrow.

● Fixed a● Fixed b● Tangent

Measure an invisible phase

A retarder shifts one Jones amplitude relative to the other. Track the measured Stokes components on the S₁ = 0 section of the Poincaré sphere.

Listen to a zero divisor

Mix a sine with a copy delayed by 1 ms. Choose a frequency, then compare the direct tone with the sum and difference outputs. At full echo gain, one output can fall completely silent.
