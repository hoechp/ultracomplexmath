# Rank of a powered convolution window

Let the ground field have characteristic zero. Choose integers $r,q\ge1$, $d\ge0$, $p\ge1$, with $0\le c\le pd$ and $0\le q+c-r\le pd$. Set $K=1+z+\cdots+z^d$ and $D=d+1$. The $r$ by $q$ matrix of interest is

$$B_{ij}=[z^{c+j-i}]K^p,\qquad 0\le i<r,\quad 0\le j<q.$$

Coefficients outside the polynomial are zero. The construction below computes its rank from a matrix of **only $p$ columns**. At fixed power its number of integer operations is bounded independently of the window dimensions and of $d$. Integer bit costs still depend on the input.

## From the missing coefficients to moments

Write the unknown column as the reversed polynomial $V=\sum_{j=0}^{q-1}v_j z^{q-1-j}$. Row $i$ of $Bv$ is the coefficient of degree $q-1+c-i$ in $W=K^p V$. Thus $Bv=0$ exactly when $W$ is supported on the two tails

$$E=[0,a)\cup[b,e),\qquad a=q+c-r,\quad b=q+c,\quad e=q+pd.$$

Intervals here contain integer exponents. Multiplication by $K^p$ is injective. Every such tail polynomial divisible by $K^p$ has a unique quotient of degree below $q$, including the zero polynomial.

Let $L=z\,d/dz$. The roots of $K$ are the nonidentity $D$th roots of unity in a splitting field. They are simple and nonzero. At each root the conditions $L^sW=0$ for $0\le s<p$ are equivalent to vanishing of the first $p$ ordinary derivative values, starting with $W$. Indeed the leading term of $L^s$ is $z^s(d/dz)^s$ and all other terms have lower derivative order. Hence $K^p\mid W$ exactly when $K\mid L^sW$ for each $s$. For $d=0$ these conditions are vacuous and the same calculation applies.

For any polynomial $P$, its remainder modulo $z^D-1$ has as coefficients the sums in each exponent class modulo $D$. Since $z^D-1=(z-1)K$, divisibility by $K$ means this remainder of degree below $D$ is a scalar multiple of $K$. Thus the sums are equal across classes for each $L^sW$. Write $E_t=\{k\in E:k\equiv t\bmod D\}$, $m_t=|E_t|$, and $v(k)=(1,k,\ldots,k^{p-1})^T$. All classes must produce the **same** moment vector

$$y=\sum_{k\in E_t}w_k v(k),\qquad 0\le t<D.$$

Put $U_t=\operatorname{span}\{v(k):k\in E_t\}$. Distinct integer nodes stay distinct in characteristic zero. The Vandermonde rank is $\min(m_t,p)$. The map taking an admissible tail polynomial to $y$ surjects onto $\bigcap_t U_t$, since the local coefficients can be chosen independently. Its kernel is the direct sum of the local kernels. Consequently

$$\dim\ker B=\sum_t (m_t-p)_++\dim\bigcap_t U_t.$$

## A bounded exact computation

For $m_t<p$, the annihilator of $U_t$ consists of polynomials of degree below $p$ vanishing on $E_t$. Its basis is

$$x^u\prod_{k\in E_t}(x-k),\qquad 0\le u<p-m_t.$$

Stack their ascending coefficient vectors as rows of $H$. Then the common dimension is $p-\operatorname{rank} H$. A full class imposes no conditions. An empty class forces $y=0$.

For each tail endpoint $n\in\{a,b,e\}$ the number of exponents $t+iD<n$, $i\ge0$, is $\lceil(n-t)/D\rceil$. It changes only at $n\bmod D$. Cutting $[0,D)$ at these three residues gives at most four segments. Within each, the local nodes are simultaneous translates and $m_t=m$ is constant. For a fixed shift $u$ each coordinate of the annihilator row is a polynomial of degree at most $m$ in the translation. By interpolation its row span over a segment is generated by the first $\min(\text{width},m+1)$ translates. Thus $H$ needs at most $4p^2$ rows and $p$ columns, even for enormous windows. Elimination on integer rows gives the exact rational rank and hence the rank over any characteristic-zero field.

## Explicit classification for the third power

Set $p=3$. Let $S$ denote the common dimension in the table. The rank is $q-\sum_t(m_t-3)_+-S$. Classes of size at least three impose no condition.

| Short residue classes | $S$ |
| --- | ---: |
| Any empty class | 0 |
| Exactly one singleton, all other classes full | 1 |
| A singleton and any other short nonempty class | 0 |
| No short classes | 3 |
| Exactly one pair, all other classes full | 2 |
| At least two pairs, no other short classes | 1 if all points $(a+b,ab)$ from pairs $\{a,b\}$ are collinear, otherwise 0 |

A singleton spans a line through $(1,k,k^2)$. Distinct singleton lines intersect at zero. The line also meets the span of another class's pair only at zero, by the three-node Vandermonde determinant. The plane for a pair has normal $(ab,-(a+b),1)$. Distinct pairs give distinct points because their sum and product determine their unordered roots. With at least two pairs the normal rank is two exactly when all the points are collinear, including vertical lines. The determinant test can use the first three pairs per constant segment by the same degree-two interpolation argument.

For the Jordan parameters $(m,n,d,p,k)=(5,15,4,3,16)$ the block has $(r,q,c)=(4,4,1)$. Its rows are $(3,6,10,15)$, $(1,3,6,10)$, $(0,1,3,6)$, $(0,0,1,3)$. The vector $(-1,3,-3,1)$ is in the kernel. The lower-left three-by-three minor has determinant one, so the rank is exactly three.

```python
from examples.moment_ranks import moment_band_rank, third_power_band_rank

assert third_power_band_rank(4, 4, 4, 1) == 3
assert moment_band_rank(4, 4, 4, 3, 1) == 3
assert moment_band_rank(10**30, 10**30, 10**20, 5, 0) == 10**30
```

## Scope, literature and reproduction

[Noferini, §4.3, Problem 4.19](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.08399v5#S4.SS3) asks when the rank loss for a quadruple of Jordan parameters is positive and how large it is. For given parameters one can apply the reduction to each admissible graded block and sum the defects. For equal Jordan sizes and even powers, a [Gram minor and total rank formula](equal-even-rank.md) now remove the sum. A direct classification across all quadruples remains open here. The [first-power result](first-power-rank.md) already performs that last step for $p=1$. The [second-power proof](boundary-rank.md) gives a shorter special-case formula.

Toeplitz minors also admit skew-Schur formulas, as in [Maximenko and Moctezuma-Salazar](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.08067). The derivation above uses classical divisibility, moments and interpolation. Publication priority for this compressed formulation and independent specialist review remain open.

For powers three through six the full run checks 9,539 Jordan blocks, 11,980 additional corner windows, 196 deliberately deficient windows and 35 complete monomial-basis operator powers.
